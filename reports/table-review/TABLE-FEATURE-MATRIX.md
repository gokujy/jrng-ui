## Reference conclusion

PrimeNG and Optimus both demonstrate mature breadth, especially browser-measured frozen/resize and
virtual-lazy behavior. JRNG deliberately keeps its one-table architecture and typed column model.
The matrix does not claim source parity or full behavioral parity where real-browser or advanced
interaction coverage is absent.
